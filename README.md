# news-image-20261124

A one-image newspaper front page, "THE WORLD TODAY", summing up the world news of Tuesday, 24 November 2026: Nvidia bringing back Jensen Huang as CEO as the lead, plus six more stories from Ukraine, Sudan, the US, the UN and India–Nepal and a four-item news ticker. The page and its flat-vector illustrations are drawn from scratch with Pillow in `render.py`, and `main.py` (standard library only) copies the rendered `entry.png` to the output directory.


To redraw the image (needs Pillow and the DejaVu fonts): `python3 render.py`
